Tensonic has launched the TS-A2S digital stereo amplifier, integrating a DAC and amplifier into a single compact chassis, aimed at small-to-medium spaces (offices, storefronts, a single zone within a multi-zone system) as a main-channel amplifier.

Built around the Texas Instruments PCM5102A DAC paired with a low-power, high-efficiency Class D amplifier, it's rated at 100W @ 6Ω with roughly 90% efficiency, keeping power consumption low during extended operation. Inputs span 2× analogue RCA, 1× 3.5mm analogue input, and both optical and coaxial digital inputs (supporting S/PDIF at 32–96kHz) — digital sources can be decoded through the built-in DAC and amplified directly, with no separate source device needed.

The unit includes professional dynamic DSP processing and a CNC-machined aluminium faceplate, with a compact footprint (215×320×98mm, 3kg) suited to space-constrained installations.

## Applications

A main-channel amplifier for small-to-medium spaces, or a single zone within a multi-zone background-music system requiring its own amplification.
