Tensonic has launched the SR103 digital recorder/player, built on an ARM-architecture system-on-chip paired with a VLSI decoding chip. It's designed to offer both performance and convenience for home karaoke recording and meeting logs, and can also integrate into a paging system for preparing scheduled announcement content or logging live paging.

Recording covers four MP3 bitrates (128K / 192K / 256K / 320K bps), with automatic chapter division and sound-activated recording; one-touch recording auto-names files, while power-loss file protection and clipping distortion alerts reduce the risk of operator error or data loss during recording. Playback supports the common formats MP3, WMA, WAV, AAC, OGG, and FLAC, with programmable playback, A-B repeat, and a spectral display; recording SNR reaches 106dB with a 102dB dynamic range.

The microphone input is a dynamic-type 6.3mm jack (+40dB gain). Outputs offer both a fixed mode (0dBV / 1Vrms) and a variable pre-out mode (−90dBV to 0dBV), with a built-in headphone output for on-site monitoring, an IR remote, and a 128×64 STN LCD display.

## Applications

Home karaoke recording, meeting and event recording for record-keeping, and as a recording front-end for preparing scheduled paging content.
