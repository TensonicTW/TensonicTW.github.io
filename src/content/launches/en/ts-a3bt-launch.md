Tensonic has launched the TS-A3BT digital music player, positioned as a "complete audio solution for small-to-medium spaces," integrating a media player, a Hi-End-grade DAC (Texas Instruments PCM5102A), and a high-efficiency Class D stereo amplifier in a single chassis rated at 100W×2 @ 6Ω.

Source support spans USB drive playback, Bluetooth, FM radio, analogue Aux input, computer USB audio output, and optical/coaxial digital inputs, with playback formats including WAV, MP3, FLAC, and APE (from storage up to 32GB) — covering nearly every common source type and operating independently without a separate source device. This makes it particularly suited to space-constrained venues that want a single unit covering both playback and amplification.

The unit includes an 8x oversampling digital filter and professional dynamic DSP processing, along with automatic overcurrent, overheat, and low-voltage protection reset for stability during extended operation. It can be operated from the front panel, an IR remote, or a mobile app, lowering the operating skill required on site.

## Applications

A complete single-unit audio solution for small-to-medium commercial spaces (storefronts, offices, dining venues), or a zone within a multi-zone background-music system needing its own independent source and amplification.
