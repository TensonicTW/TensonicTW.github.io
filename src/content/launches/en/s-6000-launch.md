Tensonic has launched the S-6000, a Taiwan-developed all-digital KTV integrated amplifier aimed at private rooms, community halls, and small banquet venues — spaces where floor area is limited but sound quality and ease of operation both matter.

The S-6000's most distinctive feature is its 25cm-deep chassis, an unusually compact form factor for this category that makes it easier to install in space-constrained venues. It's rated at 300W and provides 4 independent speaker output terminals, meaning that power isn't concentrated on a single speaker group — it can be allocated across a main front-of-room zone and a background zone according to the room's layout, giving more flexibility in power distribution.

The control interface is a 7-inch capacitive touchscreen with 10 built-in scene-based sound profiles selectable at a touch for singing, movie-watching, or music-listening. On the processing side, the S-6000 offers DSP echo, reverb, harmony, and vocal-removal effects, plus automatic feedback suppression — addressing microphone howling, one of the most common complaints in karaoke venues.

Source inputs span analogue RCA, optical, coaxial, Bluetooth V5.4, and USB, with an optional HDMI module (3-in/1-out with ARC) for integrated audio-video display needs. Control connectivity includes IR, external trigger, RS485, Wi-Fi, and RJ-45, making it straightforward to integrate into existing central-control or venue-management systems.

## Applications

Karaoke private rooms, community activity centers, small banquet venues, and any space requiring zoned front/rear playback.
