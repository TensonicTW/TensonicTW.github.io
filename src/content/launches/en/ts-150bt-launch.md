Tensonic has launched the TS-150BT commercial PA broadcasting amplifier, targeting fixed broadcast needs in medium-sized shops, offices, and institutions. A common pain point in these venues is a large speaker count across complex zoning, where a traditional low-impedance amplifier requires calculating impedance matching one speaker at a time — adding real complexity to installation and maintenance.

The TS-150BT is rated at 150W @ 4Ω and supports 100V constant-voltage output, letting speakers be wired in parallel on a single line through line transformers, driving ten 15W high-impedance speakers or fifteen 10W speakers depending on tap settings — substantially simplifying wiring for multi-speaker installs. The unit has a built-in USB, Bluetooth, FM radio, and PC USB audio media player module, serving as an independent background-music source without a separate playback device.

Microphone input is provided through 3× 6.3mm cardioid dynamic connectors, with channel #1 supporting priority broadcast that automatically overrides background music for emergency announcements or important notices. Power supports both AC 100V and DC 24V (vehicle battery) modes, suiting both fixed installation sites and mobile broadcast vehicles.

## Applications

Fixed broadcast systems for medium-sized shops, offices, schools, and institutions, and mobile broadcast applications requiring dual power support.
