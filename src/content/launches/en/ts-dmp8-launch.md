Tensonic has launched the TS-DMP8 high-resolution master digital player, targeting fixed-installation and background-music systems where the player serves as the source. A common problem in these deployments is a mismatch between source fidelity and downstream equipment — a weak source undermines even a well-specified amplifier and speaker chain.

At the core of the TS-DMP8 is the Japanese AKM AK4490EQ digital-to-analogue converter, measured at 0.002% total harmonic distortion (A-weighted), with a dynamic range of 112dB analogue / 120dB digital and a frequency response of 20Hz–20kHz (±3dB). Paired with a toroidal transformer and low-noise linear power regulation, the design goal is to minimise noise and distortion at the source so downstream systems can faithfully reproduce the original recording's detail.

Format support spans lossless WAV and FLAC (up to 24-bit/192kHz), lossless APE, and standard MP3. Three output types — analogue RCA, optical TOSLINK, and coaxial — connect directly to existing amplifiers, multi-zone systems, or DSP processors without requiring rewiring.

## Applications

Fixed background-music installations, multi-zone paging sources, and commercial audio systems requiring long, stable playback.
