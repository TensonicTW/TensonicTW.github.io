Tensonic has launched the TS-DAC standalone digital-to-analogue converter, addressing a common need: upgrading the sound quality of existing source equipment. Many users already have a computer, TV, or player serving as a source, but its built-in analogue output quality is limited — replacing the entire source chain is costly, and the TS-DAC offers a more economical middle path.

Built around the Texas Instruments PCM5102A DAC, digital inputs (optical/coaxial) achieve 112dB SNR and dynamic range with THD+N as low as −93dB. The unit uses isolated digital and analogue circuit design to reduce cross-interference between the two, keeping actual output quality close to the chip's theoretical specifications.

Alongside PRE Out and DAC Out analogue outputs, the unit includes a built-in headphone amplifier (150mW, for 16–32Ω headphones) for direct near-field or desktop headphone listening. Its compact footprint (125×175×53mm, 500g) suits placement on a desk or in a tight equipment rack.

## Applications

A sound-quality upgrade front-end for existing source equipment (computer, TV, player), or as a desktop headphone monitoring system.
