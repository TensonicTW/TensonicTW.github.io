Tensonic has launched the TS-300BT commercial PA broadcasting amplifier, the high-power model in its constant-voltage broadcast amplifier line, targeting medium-to-large commercial spaces, offices, and institutions — spacious environments with higher ambient noise.

The TS-300BT is rated at 300W @ 4Ω and supports 100V constant-voltage output, driving thirty 10W high-impedance speakers or twenty 15W speakers depending on tap settings. For larger sites with more zones than a single unit's power can cover, the TS-300BT supports parallel rear-amplifier expansion, letting total system power scale up in stages as needed rather than requiring a full amplifier replacement.

The unit has a built-in USB, Bluetooth, FM radio, and PC USB audio media player module as an independent background-music source, and microphone input through 4× 6.3mm wired connectors, with channel #1 supporting priority broadcast. The power supply accepts a wide AC 100–240V range, compatible with different regional voltage standards — well suited to cross-border deployments.

## Applications

Fixed broadcast systems for medium-to-large commercial spaces, offices, schools, and institutions — particularly sites with many zones or plans for future expansion.
