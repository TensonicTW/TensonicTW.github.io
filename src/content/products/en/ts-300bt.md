The TS-300BT is the high-power model in Tensonic's constant-voltage broadcast amplifier line, rated at 300W @ 4Ω and designed for medium-to-large commercial spaces, offices, and institutions — spacious environments with higher ambient noise. It supports 100V constant-voltage output and, depending on speaker tap settings, can drive thirty 10W high-impedance speakers or twenty 15W speakers, with the option to expand total system power further through parallel rear-amplifier connections.

The unit has a built-in USB, Bluetooth, FM radio, and PC USB audio media player module as an independent background-music source. Microphone input is provided through 4× 6.3mm wired connectors, with channel #1 supporting priority broadcast. It can be operated from the front panel, an IR remote, or a mobile app. The power supply accepts a wide AC 100–240V range, compatible with different regional voltage standards, and includes an active cooling fan for heat dissipation during extended high-power operation.
