The TS-DMP8 is Tensonic's fixed-source player for installed and background-music systems, built around the Japanese AKM AK4490EQ digital-to-analogue converter, paired with a toroidal transformer and low-noise linear power regulation to deliver near master-grade fidelity in fixed-source applications.

The faceplate is CNC-machined aluminium, and the front TFT LCD (400×240) shows playback information and album artwork. The right-side panel integrates an SD card slot and a USB port, supporting direct playback from SDXC cards (8–256GB, FAT32) and USB drives, with CUE Splitter compatibility for managing single-file master recordings split by track.

Supported audio formats span lossless WAV and FLAC (up to 24-bit/192kHz), lossless APE, and standard MP3, covering most source material. Three output types — analogue RCA, optical, and coaxial — allow pairing with existing amplifiers or digital signal processors.

## Recommended Pairing

In installed and background-music systems, the TS-DMP8 can serve as a single source feeding multiple zones. Its coaxial/optical outputs also suit multi-zone amplifiers or DSP processors with digital inputs. Confirm actual pairing recommendations with sales for site-specific requirements.
