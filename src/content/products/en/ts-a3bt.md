The TS-A3BT is positioned as a "complete audio solution for small-to-medium spaces," integrating a media player, a Hi-End-grade DAC (Texas Instruments PCM5102A), and a high-efficiency Class D stereo amplifier in a single chassis rated at 100W×2 @ 6Ω. Source support spans USB drive playback, Bluetooth, FM radio, analogue Aux input, computer USB audio output, and optical/coaxial digital inputs — covering nearly every common source type without requiring a separate source device.

Playback formats include WAV, MP3, FLAC, and APE (from storage up to 32GB), with a digital-input SNR of 112dB and THD+N as low as −93dB. The unit includes an 8x oversampling digital filter and professional dynamic DSP processing, along with automatic overcurrent, overheat, and low-voltage protection reset for stability during long operating hours. It can be operated from the front panel, an IR remote, or a mobile app.
