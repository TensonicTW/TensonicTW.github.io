The SR103 is built on an ARM-architecture system-on-chip paired with a VLSI decoding chip, designed to offer both performance and convenience for home karaoke recording and meeting logs. It can also integrate into a paging system for preparing scheduled announcement content or logging live paging.

Recording covers four MP3 bitrates (128K / 192K / 256K / 320K bps), with automatic chapter division and sound-activated recording; one-touch recording auto-names files, while power-loss file protection and clipping distortion alerts reduce the risk of operator error or data loss during recording. Playback supports the common formats MP3, WMA, WAV, AAC, OGG, and FLAC, with programmable playback, A-B repeat, and a spectral display.

The microphone input is a dynamic-type 6.3mm jack (+40dB gain), with a recording gain range of −15dB to +12dB and a recording SNR of 106dB. Outputs offer both a fixed mode (0dBV / 1Vrms) and a variable pre-out mode (−90dBV to 0dBV), plus a built-in 45mW @ 32Ω headphone output for on-site monitoring.

The SR103 works well as a recording front-end for paging systems — pre-recorded scheduled announcements can then be played back on a fixed schedule through the main PA unit.
