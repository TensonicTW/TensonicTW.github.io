The TS-150BT is a mid-power constant-voltage broadcast amplifier designed for medium-sized shops, offices, and institutions, rated at 150W @ 4Ω with 100V constant-voltage output support. Depending on the speaker tap settings used on site, it can drive ten 15W high-impedance speakers or fifteen 10W speakers — multiple speakers can be wired in parallel on the same line without calculating impedance matching for each one.

The unit has a built-in USB, Bluetooth, FM radio, and PC USB audio media player module, serving as an independent source for background-music playback without a separate source device. Microphone input is provided through 3× 6.3mm cardioid dynamic connectors, with channel #1 supporting priority broadcast — automatically overriding background-music playback for emergency announcements or important notices. Power supports both AC 100V and DC 24V (vehicle battery) modes, suiting both fixed installations and mobile broadcast vehicles, with an active cooling fan for stability during extended operation.
