The TS-A2S is a compact integrated unit combining a DAC with an amplifier, built around the Texas Instruments PCM5102A DAC paired with a low-power, high-efficiency Class D amplifier rated at 100W @ 6Ω with roughly 90% efficiency — suited to small-to-medium spaces as a main-channel amplifier, or as the drive source for one zone in a multi-zone system.

Inputs span 2× analogue RCA (for a CD/Blu-ray player and a TV/set-top box respectively), 1× 3.5mm analogue input for portable devices, and both optical and coaxial digital inputs supporting S/PDIF at 32–96kHz — digital sources can be decoded through the built-in DAC and amplified directly, with no separate source device needed. The unit includes professional dynamic DSP processing and a CNC-machined aluminium faceplate.
