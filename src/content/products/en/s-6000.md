The S-6000 is a Taiwan-developed, all-digital KTV integrated amplifier with a chassis just 25cm deep — an unusually compact form factor for this category, making it easier to install in space-constrained private rooms or event venues. Ten built-in scene-based sound profiles cover singing, movie-watching, and music-listening use cases, selectable at a touch via the 7-inch capacitive touchscreen.

On the processing side, the S-6000 provides DSP echo, reverb, harmony, and vocal-removal effects, along with automatic feedback suppression to reduce microphone squeal. 3D surround and dynamic bass enhancement round out the listening experience. Source inputs span analogue RCA, optical, coaxial, Bluetooth V5.4, and USB, with an optional HDMI module (3-in/1-out with ARC) for integrated audio-video setups.

Control connectivity is comprehensive — IR remote, external trigger, RS485, Wi-Fi, and wired RJ-45 — making it straightforward to integrate into existing central-control systems or venue management platforms.
