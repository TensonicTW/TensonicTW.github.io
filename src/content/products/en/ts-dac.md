The TS-DAC is a standalone high-resolution digital-to-analogue converter built around the Texas Instruments PCM5102A DAC, with isolated digital and analogue circuits. Digital inputs (optical/coaxial) achieve 112dB SNR and dynamic range with THD+N as low as −93dB, making it a sound-quality upgrade front-end for existing source equipment — a computer, TV, or player.

Alongside PRE Out and DAC Out analogue outputs, the unit includes a built-in headphone amplifier rated at 150mW for 16–32Ω headphones, suited to direct headphone monitoring or near-field listening. The faceplate is CNC-engraved aluminium, and the compact footprint (125×175×53mm, 500g) suits desktop placement.
